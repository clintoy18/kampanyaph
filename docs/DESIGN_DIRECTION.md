# Kampanya design direction

The existing Phase 0 direction favored an editorial workspace. The Kampanya brief adds Filipino market context and suggests deep navy, warm cream, coral-orange, and restrained teal. Choose a direction before building authentication or feature screens.

## A. Campaign atelier — recommended

Use deep navy for navigation and primary headings, warm cream for the workspace, coral for primary actions, and teal only for status or supporting emphasis. Pair a characterful serif display face with a clear sans-serif interface face; keep body copy readable and forms compact. Use a 4/8 px spacing rhythm and narrow reading measures for strategy and content. Desktop navigation groups Business, Strategy, Content, Reviews, and Campaigns around a persistent business switcher; mobile uses a concise menu with the current business always visible. Prioritize the content editor and review queue over summary cards. Provide visible focus, field errors, disabled/loading states, and a calendar list fallback.

## B. Operations ledger

Use slate and white with a single terracotta accent, compact sans-serif typography, and stronger table/list structure. This favors frequent review and scheduling work but offers less warmth for a small-business creative workspace. The same accessible navigation and state requirements apply.

Direction A best carries the approved editorial concept into the new Kampanya identity. The owner provisionally approved Direction A for this session. Use authentic labels and examples, not invented metrics or testimonials. No dark mode is proposed.
