description: Formula-guided OpenAI content generation
---

Treat OpenAI as an external service behind a provider adapter, even though only OpenAI is initially enabled. Build prompts using **verified** business context, audience, marketing objective, hook, value proposition, evidence-backed trust element, CTA, and platform optimization. Missing proof means omit trust claim or ask for evidence; never fabricate testimonials or performance figures. Enforce structured output validation, output length limits, timeouts/retries, error handling, rate limits, and usage/cost tracking. Record prompt-template version, model, method, inputs reference, and generation metadata without unnecessarily storing secrets. Distinguish production formula-guided generation from later controlled conventional-prompt research comparison. In research comparisons hold model and scenario inputs constant; seek adviser-approved criteria before implementing evaluation dashboards. AI outputs always editable and reviewable; never auto-publish.
