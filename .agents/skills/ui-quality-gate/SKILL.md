---
name: ui-quality-gate
description: Visual and functional quality gates
---

For each delivered screen, verify at ~375px, ~768px, and ~1440px widths; no overflow, clipping or unusable dialogs. Keyboard-only navigation and focus visibility; accessible names, field errors, contrast and reduced motion. Verify loading, empty, error, unauthorized, long text, and slow network states. Test critical flows: business switch, draft edit, review/approval, calendar. Report screenshots or actual preview results, defects, and remediation. Never mark passed checks without running them.
